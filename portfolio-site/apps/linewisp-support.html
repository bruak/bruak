Linewisp support

Network and device measurements, with the controls to make them your own.

Keep readings close

  1. On Network, choose Start live window.
  2. Return Home and keep the window playing. Slide it past the screen edge to tuck it away; leave the handle visible.
  3. Drag the handle inward to bring it back. Avoid Close or Pause while you want updates.

iOS controls background execution and may interrupt it. Dynamic Island alone does not keep network sampling running. Tap the island to reopen Linewisp and refresh. In the expanded island and on the Lock Screen, layouts needing new samples also offer a Refresh link.

A stopwatch or countdown can keep moving without new network readings. Timer-only layouts therefore omit the sample-refresh header. The system also chooses which activities appear in Dynamic Island; the island may not appear alongside the live window.

Understand your readings

  • Upload is on the left; download is on the right. Mbps measures megabits per second; MB/s measures megabytes per second. Eight bits make one byte.
  • Traffic totals cover measured intervals. They are not your carrier’s allowance or bill. Gaps are marked rather than filled with estimates.
  • Network tools do not need location. Coordinates, movement speed, altitude and compass tools need permission and a recent signal.
  • No ping reply does not prove an outage. A network can block ICMP diagnostics.
  • FPS belongs to Linewisp’s own visible screen. Thermal state is the state reported by iOS, not a temperature measurement.

History, export and permissions

Choose End session, open history, then use History actions → Save CSV file to save a measurement. Delete individual sessions or all history in the app. Recovery copies are cleared too; previously exported files and iOS backups remain under your control.

Change location and notification permissions in iOS Settings. Countdown completion alerts are optional local notifications. Close the live window when finished; a slower sampling cadence and fewer active instruments reduce work.

Get help

In Settings → Support and about, preview the details to share, then choose Copy support details. Include your iOS version, what you expected and the steps that reproduce the problem. The copied details exclude location, IP addresses and measurement history and are not sent automatically.

Please do not send passwords, API keys or personal screenshots. You choose what to share by email.

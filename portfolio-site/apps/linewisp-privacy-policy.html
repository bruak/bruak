Privacy policy

Linewisp · iPhone

Linewisp · Last updated: 7 November 2026

Linewisp is developed by Burak Soykan. It has no account or application server, and uses no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDK. The app does not automatically send the developer your usage, location, device details or measurement history.

Support and help

Data on your device

Layouts, favorites, timers and up to 200 completed network measurements are stored in the app on your device. Your iOS backups and files you export are managed by the systems and services you choose.

Permissions you control

Location permission is used for coordinates, movement speed, altitude and compass instruments. An active location view or window may update in the background, with the iOS location indicator. Network instruments do not request location.

Notification permission is requested only for optional countdown completion alerts. You can change these permissions in iOS Settings.

Network diagnostics

When network diagnostics are active, ICMP probes are sent to one.one.one.one. DNS and destination network providers can see your connection’s IP address as part of servicing requests. Packets contain no location, measurement history or persistent device identifier. The developer does not receive user-specific diagnostic logs from these providers.

Exports and support

CSV export is initiated by you and includes measurement dates, duration, Wi-Fi/cellular byte totals and gap status. It excludes location, raw device boot time and disk-space readings.

Support details are previewed before you copy them and exclude location, IP addresses and measurement history. Nothing is sent automatically; you decide what to include when contacting support.

If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to send are received through the email service used for that correspondence. They are used to respond to your request. Do not include passwords, credentials or unnecessary personal information. Contact us if you want us to delete a support conversation; any applicable legal obligations and the email provider’s retention practices may also apply.

Deleting local data

You can delete history records individually or together, including their recovery copies. Deleting the app removes its local data. Manage previously exported files and system backups wherever you saved them. There is no Linewisp account to close.

This website and policy updates

This policy describes the Linewisp iPhone app. These support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and load fonts from Google Fonts; those services receive the normal connection information needed to serve the pages and fonts. Visiting this website does not send the app’s measurements to the developer.

If the app’s practices change, this policy will be updated with a new date.
